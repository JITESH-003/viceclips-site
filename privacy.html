Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Vice Clips Uploader is used only by the owner of the Vice Clips channel to upload videos to their own YouTube channel via the YouTube Data API.

  • The app requests only the youtube.upload permission.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never shared.
  • No data from other users is collected, stored, sold or shared.
  • Access can be revoked anytime at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
